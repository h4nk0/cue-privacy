Cue Privacy Policy

Cue: Daily Routines · Last updated 28 October 2026

Cue is built to remember your routines for you, not to collect anything about you.

What Cue collects

Nothing. Cue has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your information to the developer or to any third party.

Where your routines are kept

Your routines, steps, Cues, settings and the history of what you've done are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

If iCloud is turned on, Cue also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud account so your devices stay in sync. That copy is held by Apple under your Apple Account, and the developer cannot see it. See Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications, so Cue can remind you when a routine or Cue is due. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Alarms, only if you turn on "Ring like an alarm", so a routine can ring even on silent.

You can turn either off at any time in the Settings app.

Opening other apps

If you choose a step that opens another app or link when it starts, Cue simply opens it. Anything you do there is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting a routine in Cue deletes it from your devices and your iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes everything on that device. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

Cue does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
